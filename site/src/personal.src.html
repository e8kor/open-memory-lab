You decide what sticks

Until you approve a memory, your other tools never see it, so a wrong guess doesn't spread. The Review page, /web/review, shows them one at a time, with anything the scan flagged; on a server you run, oml review list lets you approve a batch in one act.

{{GLYPH:gate}}

Kept to your workarea

Your memories live in a workarea of your own, which other people's keys read only if granted. On the hosted server its operator can read them; on a server you run, they stay with you. A pasted API key or private key is refused, not saved.

Connect claude.ai and your phone in three steps

  1. Get a code. Sign in at /web, open Keys, issue a connector key and copy its connect code. It works once, for ten minutes.
  2. Add the connector. On claude.ai, open Customize > Connectors and add a custom connector at https://memory.doublegate.io/mcp.
  3. Paste and confirm. Paste the code on the page that opens. Every Claude app on that account, your phone included, can then use it.

Claude's Free plan allows one custom connector. Connecting is verified live; a full remember and recall from claude.ai is not yet (what we have not tested live). Using Claude Code? Run the two commands in the terminal box, then /reload-plugins.
