**Web console, Review.** The Rust SDK's `Memory` reads the fields the API serves and the console dropped: `kind`, `provenance`, `origin`, `retirement`, `stale`, `source_checked`, `derives_from`, `attachments`, `tokens`, `unreviewed`, `held_expires_at` and `plaintext_digest`; its `AdminRead` reads `held_by`, `promoted_at` and `scan_finding` (design notes row 10; ADR-0070 d9). No API changes.

**Web console, one card for every memory.** Memories rows, the Review lists of both pages, the served review item and the one-memory page of both pages draw one card (`crates/clients/oml-web/src/card.rs`; the design system's `MemoryCard` and `ReviewCard`). A row's summary holds the head line alone (the state, or in Review the kind of decision: New memory, Edit of vN, Retirement of vN), and the facts sit outside it in one order and wording on every page: the author and its kind, `same user` where the held listing marks it, the workarea's reference, the claim key and version, what it replaces or retires, its origin kind, its scan findings, and the instant it was written. Opened, it adds the format, the source with its last check, valid time, the approval as ADR-0077 words it (never who), and where it is served. The page adds the ids and the change against the replaced version (one more read), and keeps the Mermaid frame. The Memories row keeps its version bar and provenance timeline (design notes row 12, not decided). The admin's One memory page now shows the scan finding the person's page shows, and says why it offers no verdict.

**Web console, Review.** The person's Review lists what waits for the person's reviewer key: grouped by workarea, from one read of `GET /v2/review/held` (exact up to 200, then "200 or more"), as cards whose rows mark the person's own agents' writes `same user`. Each row has **Open to judge**, which draws the item with the verdict form and records a one-id listed-set verdict through `POST /v2/review/verdicts/bulk` (ADR-0055 d4; the operator decided on 2026-11-13, design notes rows 1 and 2). A version the reviewer key wrote, or one in its lineage, is never listed, and judging its id is refused with `review.author_in_lineage`. The admin's bulk review draws the same cards with a pick box in place of the nine-column table. Post/Redirect/Get with counts is unchanged (row 11). Copy: "Nothing waits for review.", "this reviewer key may not judge it", "a confirmation comes first", and " (you)" becomes the `same user` chip.
