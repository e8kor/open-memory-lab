**Web console.** Every page is drawn on one of two sides, and its bar says which (the design system's BrandHeader, variant A; ADR-0069). The Personal bar is the plain bar with the scope word Personal and its glyph; the Administration bar changes ground and ink places, closes with a heavy star-coloured rule and carries the word Administration and its glyph. Each side's `<nav>` is labelled with its word and holds that side's pages alone: Start, Memories, Review, Atlas, Keys, Workareas, Guardrails; and People, Quotas, Memories, Review, Atlas, Keys, Workareas, Curate, where the admin Atlas is now a tab of its own. The Administration and Your page tabs are gone: one switch after the nav leads to the other side, drawn on the Personal bar only for a session that holds the admin capacity. The brand leads to the side's first page. Titles read `Memories · OML` and `Admin · Memories · OML`, a page below a tab putting its own name first, and each page's heading is its tab's word: Quotas (was Users), Atlas (was Memories) and Start (was Connect an agent). A refusal page is drawn inside the bar of the side it happened on, the Personal bar for a person without the admin capacity on an admin address, with one link back named by the tab of the page that refused. The bar stays pinned at every width, wraps its nav instead of scrolling it, and keeps the scope word, the person's name and the organisation on a phone. Two glyphs, `personal` and `admin`, join `design/glyphs`.

**Web console and API wording.** The console's copy is impersonal, at the operator's request of 2026-11-13: no "you", "your" or "my", and "the user" or "the admin" where an actor is needed. The footer reads "Every act on these pages is journalled under one of this account's keys."; the Start page's steps read "Choose a client", "Get a credential" and **Issue credential**, its account section "Account" and **Export memories**, its usage card "Usage"; a denied web session reads "This web session is restricted: it cannot issue keys, get connect codes or cut off keys. An administrator can."; the sign-in and sign-up pages drop the second person, and the connect page does too, its warning `ONLY_IF_YOURS` now reading "Enter the code only on a page this account's user opened, by adding the connector in Claude, in the last few minutes. A link from anyone else may connect their agent instead; if someone else sent this link, close it." (the operator's decision of 2026-11-13). Four API messages change their wording, not their code or details: `limit.quota` "the {quota} quota of {limit} is reached", `access.not_found` "this session holds no {capacity} key on this organisation", `request.validation` "an issued key is a reader or a writer" and "an issued key is named {user}-<name>", and `expected_version` "required: the claim's latest version as last read".
