- **Recall can search the trusted workareas a key already reads, and never widens what it may read** ([ADR-0072](adr/ADR-0072-recall-searches-the-trusted-workareas-a-key-already-reads-chosen-per-query-by-reviewed-descriptors-and-never-widens-what-it-may-read.md) phase 1, accepted; issue #205). Recall and `oml_read` take `sources`: `"scope"`, the default and today's answer, `"wide"`, which adds the `committed` and `validated` workareas the key reads outside its scope, or a list of workareas searched alone, answered whole or refused. It is off until a tenant admin turns federation on (`GET` and `POST /v2/federation`, `oml search federation`, journalled `federation.policy_set`); a person's reviewer key or an admin may stop recalls scoped elsewhere from searching a workarea (`POST /v2/workareas/{workarea}/federation`, `oml workareas federation`, journalled `workarea.federation_set`), and the workarea read serves `federation.accept_federated`. Below `max_sources` (8) `wide` searches every such workarea; above it, those holding the largest share of the query's matching rows, ranked by exact per-workarea counts with ties by name. Every hit is scored with statistics taken over the scope and every candidate before any selection, and a fused pipeline reads its lists over them too, so no hit's score depends on which workareas were searched. Answers name what was searched (`search.sources`, each hit's `via`), and a `scope` answer of a federated tenant carries `search.widen` when a workarea the key reads elsewhere holds the query's words at twice the scope's rate. Refusals name their field: `/sources`, `/search` for `bm25-sparse`, and `/sources/<i>`, where an unreadable and an absent name answer the same `access.not_found`. Migration 0040 adds the policy columns and `recall_events.sources`, `federated_workarea_ids` and `reference_workarea_ids`; two metrics, `oml_recall_sources_total{mode}` and `oml_recall_federated_hits_total`, and two doctor lines join. The ten phase 1 leakage and invariance laws, with laws for held rows, the list's refusal order and the policies' authorisation, run on the in-memory double and on PostgreSQL, `eval/recall/federated.sh` measures `wide` against `scope` and the union, and `oml-pg`'s `federation_candidates` example reports how many trusted workareas each live key reads. The migration lint now flags a CHECK written inline on an added column. The SDKs, openapi, the MCP tool and the generated references are updated.
