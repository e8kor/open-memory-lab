**Proposed: skills, documents, the workarea builder and the Memories cockpit (#374).** Four proposed records, for the operator's acceptance after the cockpit and builder mockups. [ADR-0083](adr/ADR-0083-the-memories-page-is-one-cockpit-with-approved-to-review-and-submitted-views-and-held-rows-never-appear-as-approved.md) makes Memories one page with Approved (promoted tips only), To review (with Judge and a count) and Submitted (the person's own held and rejected rows), adding `mine` and a count read to the API, a "Fixes to confirm" list for proposed ADR-0089's admin fixes, and moving bulk review off the admin side. [ADR-0082](adr/ADR-0082-a-person-builds-and-edits-a-workareas-skills-guardrails-and-documents-in-the-console-each-edit-a-held-version-under-the-persons-own-keys.md) adds a user-side builder whose every edit is a held version under the person's own keys, a preview route that writes nothing, and approval as for memories today: in the person's own root their review key may approve, marked `same_user`, and elsewhere another person approves. [ADR-0081](adr/ADR-0081-a-document-is-a-reviewed-markdown-or-text-claim-that-recall-searches-by-passage-and-the-mcp-server-serves-as-a-resource.md) adds kind `document`, recalled by passage and served as an MCP resource under `oml://`. [ADR-0080](adr/ADR-0080-a-skill-is-a-reviewed-claim-the-mcp-server-lists-as-a-prompt-and-serves-as-a-resource-loaded-live-and-never-copied.md) adds kind `skill`, which agents may propose, approved only by a person, listed as an MCP prompt and readable as a resource, and indexed at session start; an agent's proposed or updated skill reaches its author's agents at once, marked unreviewed; the plugin asks before a skill is written under `.claude/skills/`, since skills are kept in OML; inside an in-progress workarea held skills and guardrails reach the agents connected there marked unreviewed, as held memories do, and nowhere else.
