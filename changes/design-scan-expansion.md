- **The scan names a threat class for every finding** ([ADR-0074](adr/ADR-0074-the-scan-names-a-threat-class-for-every-finding-refuses-only-what-can-never-be-safe-holds-or-annotates-the-rest-and-recall-serves-every-hit-as-data-with-its-findings.md), proposed). Beyond secrets, a stored memory is checked for prompt injection aimed at the reading agent, hidden and confusable Unicode, encoded payloads decoded once, rendered-fetch and link exfiltration, dangerous commands in code, SSRF-shaped URLs and credentials in URL queries; only what no true memory needs refuses, heuristics hold for a person's verdict or annotate, and a new claim that contradicts an approved one is flagged with the old claim's id. Recall serves every hit with its findings and escapes hidden characters on the text surfaces; an opt-in local classifier annotates held versions off the write path; `oml-scan/2` is applied to stored memories only by an admin's recorded rescan, which never demotes and withholds a secret found after approval. A hold finding is approved only by a signed-in person's session key with a reviewer grant, in a person lane where crews refer, a person may dismiss a finding with a reason, and nothing expires; inside an in-progress workarea such a memory stays with its author. A person is confirmed and freshly signed in, by the web or, without sign-in, by a local session bound to an OS user and a second factor; a bearer admin key can neither add a person nor lower a ceiling, extend an allowlist or re-serve a withheld secret, and a solo user may approve their own agents' findings, marked `same_user`, while a team needs another person. Every rule reads the text after decoding, NFKC, case folding and the TR39 skeleton, and a tenant may lower a class's verdict within fixed floors and allowlist hosts for a limited time. A red-team corpus of benign and malicious pairs, with a held-out set and mutations, reports precision and recall per class, and the new rules' hold rate is measured on a live store's copy before people take on any load. Six phases are tracked as issues [#213](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/213) to [#218](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/218); personal data stays with [#177](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/177) and [#140](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/140).
