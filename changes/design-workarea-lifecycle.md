**Accepted: the workarea lifecycle and the constitution's three bounded exceptions.** The operator accepted five records on 2026-11-14, before they are built, every question at its recommendation, and kept [ADR-0088](adr/ADR-0088-an-imported-version-counts-as-reviewed-only-from-a-trusted-source-a-tenant-pinned-and-never-for-instructions.md) (verdicts from a trusted source server) deferred; the [workarea lifecycle review](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/blob/main/design/workarea-lifecycle.md) records the answers. [ADR-0001](adr/ADR-0001-the-constitution.md) states its organising principle, loose in development scopes and strict in production ones (committed and validated workareas, published releases and what a `latest` or pinned key reads), and lists three bounded exceptions in d5 and nothing else: the in-progress workarea, widened to the subtree's ancestors; own-root same-person approval, marked `same_user`, which was built and is now named; and the admin fixes. The development self-signing exception, never built, is retired. The third exception lets an admin key held by a person fix production at once, with a reason, marked to every reader, kept out of every release cut until confirmed, by its own person in a development scope and by a person other than the admin's own (never `same_user`) in a production one, and, separately, read claim text on the admin routes alone under an investigation's reason, which records the relaxation ADR-0045 d21 made; R1 is unchanged, its title and summaries now name the exceptions, and ADR-0010 d6 names the fix. [ADR-0089](adr/ADR-0089-an-admin-fixes-a-production-workarea-at-once-by-a-marked-break-glass-act-that-a-person-confirms-or-reverts-afterwards.md) defines the six fix acts, the person who confirms or reverts and the person-side read the To do page lists. [ADR-0085](adr/ADR-0085-a-workarea-publishes-immutable-releases-that-one-person-cuts-and-another-ratifies-and-each-key-binds-to-the-tip-the-latest-release-or-a-pinned-one.md): releases one person cuts and another ratifies, each key bound to the tip, the latest release or a pin; a cut over an unconfirmed fix is refused, a released in-progress workarea federates at its latest release, and the console acts through linked tenant keys. [ADR-0086](adr/ADR-0086-a-workareas-tests-are-reviewed-claims-the-server-runs-under-the-callers-key-and-a-release-is-published-only-through-ordered-gates.md): tests are reviewed claims the server runs under the caller's key, a release is published only through ordered gates, and any ratifier, never the proposer, overrides a failing test or a blocking impact finding with a reason through `POST /v2/workareas/{w}/releases/{proposal}/gates/{gate}/override` (#384). [ADR-0087](adr/ADR-0087-an-impact-report-shows-under-the-callers-own-reads-what-a-candidate-release-changes-for-the-workareas-that-receive-it.md): the impact report under the caller's own reads. [ADR-0084](adr/ADR-0084-a-workarea-moves-between-servers-as-a-signed-bundle-that-lands-held-or-in-progress-and-is-reviewed-again-unless-its-source-server-is-pinned.md): a workarea moves between servers as a signed bundle, always encrypted, exported by a person's key under an `own_root` default policy, landing held.
