Fixed a degraded stop under-reporting the recall counts it lost (#333, from the re-verification of bulk A; ADR-0071 d8). When the writer did not answer within five seconds, the stop told only of the counts still in the queue: counts a full queue had already dropped (with 10,501 handed over and the writer blocked, it told of 10,001) and counts the store had refused earlier in the pass in flight (three refused and two held behind a lock, it told of 2) were left for a pass that never came, so `usage_recall_counts_lost` and `oml_usage_recall_counts_lost_total` read low. Every count handed to the recall meter is now written, told as lost, or still unaccounted; the writer tells of a refused sum as it fails, and the stop tells of what is left, so what the two tell sums to at most what was accepted and never written (a writer still mid-write at the stop can commit rows the stop already told as lost), and no count is told twice. The stop's warning names its own number `told_lost_at_stop`: only what the stop told, since earlier ones are in the events and the metric. The queue and its bound are unchanged.
