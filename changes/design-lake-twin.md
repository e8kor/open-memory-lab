**Accepted: the lake becomes a replayable twin of PostgreSQL (ADR-0090).** The operator accepted the record on 2026-11-14, before it is built, with every default drafted for confirmation as written: the RPO warning at 300 seconds and failure at 3,600 until #390 measures the lag, an hourly signed head checkpoint kept off the lake, the counter images, the erase tombstone before the drain, the refused cross-tenant conflict, `key_hash_digest` and the pinned subject, and its amendments of ADR-0003, ADR-0005 and ADR-0060. PostgreSQL stays the commit point. Each commit stages, in its own transaction, the fold inputs its payload carries only by digest or not at all (bodies of every version, rules, key hashes, addresses, findings, idempotency outcomes); an erase before the drain leaves a tombstone so the tenant keeps draining; the lake writer seals them into `twin/inputs`, keeps the wrapped data keys in `twin/keys` and the usage counters in `twin/state`, and `bronze.events` becomes the twin journal. Every `oml-pg` writer is classed as journal, staged part, derived or excluded. Each tenant drains on a frontier of its own, the provider first, and an operator may waive one event with a recorded reason. `oml-lakewriter rebuild` would fence the target and the lake, refuse a cross-tenant conflict by name, verify every chain and an off-lake signed head checkpoint before writing, replay in global outbox order through a new `fold_of`, and set the outbox sequence past every frontier; a point-in-time cut forks the lake root under an epoch, a drill never feeds the writer, and a restored backup rolls forward from the lake. `oml-lakewriter compare` and two laws prove a rebuild equal to its source. Sessions, tokens, connect codes, review claims and the signing key are not carried, and connections replay revoked as `rebuilt`. It amends ADR-0003 d1, d3 and d6, ADR-0060 d2, d3, d6, d14 and d19, and ADR-0005 d3 and d4, whose inventory gains the staging table and the two twin tables and whose guarantee now names the clear text in signed payloads it cannot erase (#389). ADR-0080 to ADR-0087 and ADR-0089 each name their twin parts (d18). Measurement is #390.
