**The lake becomes a replayable twin of PostgreSQL (proposed ADR-0090).** PostgreSQL stays the commit point. Each commit stages, in its own transaction, the fold inputs its payload carries only by digest or not at all (bodies of every version, rules, key hashes, addresses, findings, idempotency outcomes); the lake writer seals them into `twin/inputs`, keeps the wrapped data keys in `twin/keys` and the usage counters in `twin/state`, and `bronze.events` becomes the twin journal. Every `oml-pg` writer is classed as journal, staged part, derived or excluded. Each tenant drains on a frontier of its own, the provider first, and an operator may waive one event with a recorded reason. `oml-lakewriter rebuild` would fence the target and the lake, verify every chain and an off-lake signed head checkpoint before writing, replay in global outbox order through a new `fold_of`, and set the outbox sequence past every frontier; a point-in-time cut forks the lake root under an epoch, a drill never feeds the writer, and a restored backup rolls forward from the lake. `oml-lakewriter compare` and two laws prove a rebuild equal to its source. Sessions, tokens, connect codes, review claims and the signing key are not carried, and connections replay revoked as `rebuilt`. It amends ADR-0003 d1, d3 and d6, ADR-0060 d2, d3, d6, d14 and d19, and ADR-0005 d3 and d4, whose inventory gains the staging table and the two twin tables and whose guarantee now names the clear text in signed payloads it cannot erase (#389). Measurement is #390.
