**The lake becomes a replayable twin of PostgreSQL (proposed ADR-0090).** PostgreSQL stays the commit point. The proposal makes `bronze.events` the twin journal that is never skipped, adds `twin/inputs` (each fold input a journal payload carries only by digest or not at all, sealed, held bodies included) and `twin/keys` (the lake's copy of the wrapped data keys), and keeps the `twin/` prefix out of every consumer zone. `oml-lakewriter rebuild` would verify each tenant's chain against the deployment's public key and replay it into an empty database, by global outbox seq or time, per tenant, or forward from a restored backup; `oml-lakewriter compare` and a law would prove the rebuilt database equal to its source. Sessions, tokens, connect codes, the signing key and rate counters stay out and are re-issued; the erase horizon of every rebuild is now. It amends ADR-0003 d1, d3 and d6, ADR-0060 d2, d6, d14 and d19, and ADR-0005 d4, and names three drifts it does not repair: the storage page's PostgreSQL frontier, the security page's keyring-wrapped DEKs, and version titles carried in clear in the journal.
