**Withdraw.** The author of a held version now withdraws it: its own key, an agent's included, over `POST /v1/memories/{artifact_id}/withdraw`, the MCP tool `oml_withdraw` and `oml withdraw <artifact_id>`, and a person from the console's Memories page, on a held row any of their keys wrote, after a confirmation. The version is kept as `withdrawn` with its text, leaves every review queue and is never served; it stays listed to its author. No verdict is recorded and no reviewer is involved. Another key's version is refused `access.author_only`, and a version that is not held `memory.not_held`, a new `memory` error family in both SDKs, which gain `withdraw()`. The act is journalled as `memory.withdrawn`, counted in `oml_memories_withdrawn_total`, and `oml doctor` fails on `memory.withdrawn_in_queue` if a withdrawn version is ever left in a review queue. The lake writer maps every stored state, `rejected` and `withdrawn` included, to a `silver.claims` state instead of dead-lettering them as `version_state_unknown` ([ADR-0083](adr/ADR-0083-the-memories-page-is-one-cockpit-with-approved-to-review-and-submitted-views-and-held-rows-never-appear-as-approved.md) d13, #401, #440).
