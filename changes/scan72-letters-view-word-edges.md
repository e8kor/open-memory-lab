Changed the injection scan's letters-only view to read a phrase only at word edges, and the rule set to `oml-scan/1.2` (#226; ADR-0074 d6, d21). The edges are read from the folded text (removable characters made a space, NFKD, lower case) as the raw rules' ASCII `\b` reads them: an ASCII letter touching a phrase makes it part of a longer word, and a digit, `_`, a letter of another script, a fullwidth form or punctuation is an edge. A phrase inside a longer word (`Danish`, `developer mode only`) or a camelCase identifier no longer holds a memory; a phrase joined by `_`, `/` or `-`, spelled out letter by letter, or set beside non-Latin text holds as before. The cost: a phrase glued to an ASCII letter (`xignore previous instructions`, `ignore previous instructionss`, `pleaseignore previous instructions`, `do.not.tell.the.users`) no longer holds, which cannot be told from `Danish`. The letters-only `new-instructions` phrase is now `new system instructions`, three words as ADR-0065 d14 asks, and holds when a colon follows on its line or it ends its line, as a heading does (so a sentence that ends in `new system instructions` with no full stop holds too); `The new system instructions are in the wiki` and the two-word `new instructions` no longer hold in the letters view (the plain-text rule keeps `new instructions:`). A false-positive corpus of 123 prose, code, identifier and URL samples joins the scanner tests: 35 of the 97 that are not meant to hold held under `/1.1` and none does under `/1.2`, while the 26 that hold by design still do. The red-team corpus gains 16 malicious samples with their 16 benign twins. **After you upgrade, run `oml admin rescan --record-held`.** The rule set's name moved, so until it has run, sibling agents in an in-progress workarea do not see held versions scanned under `oml-scan/1.1`; `oml doctor` warns with `scan_held_unrescanned` and `scan_rescan_due` meanwhile. Nothing is demoted or deleted, and the findings `/1.1` recorded on a held version, including the false positives this change removes, stay on it until a reviewer judges it.
