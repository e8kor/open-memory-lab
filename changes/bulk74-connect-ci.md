The CLI's `connect_flow` suite times each sign-in call of the connect flow (ADR-0031) over TCP against a lead started inside the test: both discovery documents, the registration, the three authorize requests and the token exchange must each answer under the 10 seconds Claude allows, and a refresh under the 30 seconds it allows a renewal. CI's Postgres test job prints each step's time in its log (#115). The suite already ran in that job under `OML_TEST_REQUIRE_SPLIT=1`, so a run that cannot mint its role pair fails rather than skipping.
