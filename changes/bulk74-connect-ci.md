The CLI's `connect_flow` suite times each sign-in call of the connect flow (ADR-0031) over TCP against a lead started inside the test: the unauthenticated `/mcp` probe, both discovery documents, the registration and the token exchange must each answer under the 10 seconds Claude allows, and a refresh under the 30 seconds it allows a renewal. The three authorize requests come from the person's browser, which no Claude limit covers; the suite holds them to the same 10 seconds by its own choice. CI's Postgres test job prints each step's time in its log and fails that step unless the one timing test ran and passed (#115). The suite already ran in that job under `OML_TEST_REQUIRE_SPLIT=1`, so a run that cannot mint its role pair fails rather than skipping.
