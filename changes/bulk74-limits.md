**Quotas.** The limits open sign-up launches with are recorded as the operator's decision of 2026-11-14: ADR-0068's defaults kept (1,000 memories, 5 keys, 5 connections, 120 requests a minute per key, 5 sign-ups an hour from one client address), `quota.text_bytes` at 50,000,000 bytes and `quota.writes_per_month` at 10,000 so that no single account can fill the disk, and a `recalls` value of 100,000 a month recorded and not enforced, since reading never blocks and no setting holds a recall limit. Every value is recalibrated after 30 days of live data under #164 ([ADR-0071](adr/ADR-0071-every-account-is-metered-by-counters-the-write-updates-in-its-own-transaction-and-named-quota-profiles-set-its-limits.md), [ADR-0068](adr/ADR-0068-anyone-signs-in-and-gets-a-private-root-under-quotas-when-the-operator-opens-sign-up.md), #180).
