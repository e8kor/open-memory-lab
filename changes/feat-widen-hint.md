- **A `scope` recall says when a wider search would find more, at no cost to the recall** ([ADR-0072](adr/ADR-0072-recall-searches-the-trusted-workareas-a-key-already-reads-chosen-per-query-by-reviewed-descriptors-and-never-widens-what-it-may-read.md) d8; issue #244). It needs federation on. A `scope` answer then carries `search.widen` when a workarea the key reads elsewhere is richer in the query's words. Richer means at least twice as often per memory as the scope, on at least two memories. The hint is read off a signal the lead builds in the background, per key and scope. The signal holds the counts a `wide` recall reads. It serves for at most 300 seconds, and only under the grants it was built with. Without one the answer carries no hint, and the lead builds it for the next recall. A `scope` recall still reads no candidate and no count; its one added read is the query's word forms. `GET /v2/federation` serves `widen_signal`: signals built, recalls unserved, failed builds and the last failure. `oml doctor` warns on a failed build, and when no signal was ever built. The MCP server instructions, openapi and the SDKs gain `search.widen` again.
