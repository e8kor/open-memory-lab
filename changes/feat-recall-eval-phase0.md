- **Phase 0 of ADR-0075, offline: generated questions do not beat `bm25-sparse` on question-shaped queries, and do not add to it** ([ADR-0075](adr/ADR-0075-each-approved-memory-is-indexed-by-the-questions-it-answers-generated-off-the-request-path-scanned-stored-with-their-generator-and-matched-only-by-an-opt-in-pipeline.md) d12, for [#235](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/235)). The operator checked 10% of the grown set's labels and added two answering memories to one vague query (g0113); the run uses that version of the set. A local doc2query T5 (`doc2query/msmarco-t5-base-v1`, not a Phi model, since a Phi model wrote the queries) generated five questions for each of the 200 memories, in 229 s on 4 CPU threads, and d6's filters kept 555 of them. Each pipeline was scored offline over migration 0020's own lexemes: `bm25-postings` reproduces the served success@5 on all 786 queries, and `bm25-sparse` on 784. On the 687 question-shaped queries, `bm25-questions` scored success@5 0.926 against `bm25-sparse`'s 0.958 (2 wins, 24 losses). `bm25-sparse` with a question field scored 0.959 (3 wins, 2 losses, p 0.50), and its 95% interval rules out an added gain above 0.7 points. By d12, v0.7.1 recommends `bm25-sparse` for question-style use, and the operator parked question indexing on 2026-11-11 ([report](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/blob/feat/recall-eval-phase0/eval/bench/results/phase0/report.md)).
