- **The web console's overhead over the API is measured** ([ADR-0070](adr/ADR-0070-the-web-console-is-a-client-of-the-public-http-api-in-a-crate-that-cannot-reach-the-application-core.md), the table of 2026-11-11; issue #170). `bench/web/run.py` times every page through `oml-web` and in process on the same data, and [Web console overhead](benchmarks/web.md) publishes it. The hop itself costs at most 4.3 ms p95, but 16 of 21 pages miss the 5 ms p95 budget by 9.5 to 42.7 ms: every page opens with two `whoami` calls of 8 to 17 ms each at the server. The per-session in-flight cap is not needed and pages get no request-rate share of their own; the Workareas views, whose calls grow with the tree, need the subtree's offers in one call instead.
