**Design: a protected or frozen workspace can be marked public.** [ADR-0098](adr/ADR-0098-a-protected-or-frozen-workspace-can-be-marked-public-and-then-every-reading-key-in-the-tenant-reads-its-approved-items.md), accepted design of 2026-11-26, decided by the operator for v0.8.6 and not built. One `public` flag on a `protected` or `frozen` node outside every user root lets every reader, contributor and reviewer key of the tenant, user keys included, read that node's approved items, with their principals and readable lineage, without a grant of its own; a submitter key still recalls nothing and an admin key reads no content. Every default recall searches every public node the key reads, ranked beside the scope's own hits and marked `via: "public"`, with or without federation; its steering and guardrails apply only on its own chain, and the crowding cost is accepted. The flag covers the node alone and children are not inherited; `universe` is eligible and keeps its no-author serving. Global denials still win, the ordering contract is unchanged, and nothing crosses tenants or is served without a key. Setting it is an admin act of class widening under ADR-0097, waiting on a second admin person, and clearing it is narrowing, at once. Amendment notes dated 2026-11-26 are added to ADR-0009 (d5), ADR-0044 (d3, d10) and ADR-0097 (d3, d5), and the workspaces, lifecycle, roles-and-keys and retrieval pages describe the design as designed, not built.
