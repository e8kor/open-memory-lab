Changed the `new system instructions` rule of the injection scan's letters-only view, still `oml-scan/1.2` since that rule set is not released (#317; ADR-0074 d6, ADR-0065 d14). A phrase with no colon after it now holds only when it is its line: nothing else stands on the line but the phrase, the marks of a heading, an emphasis, a quote, a list item, a banner or a bracket, a list number, an HTML tag and any closing punctuation. `## New system instructions`, `NEW SYSTEM INSTRUCTIONS`, `**New system instructions**`, a setext or closed ATX heading, `> New system instructions`, `- New system instructions`, `NEW SYSTEM INSTRUCTIONS!`, `New system instructions.`, `[new system instructions]`, `<<< new system instructions >>>` and `<h1>New system instructions</h1>` hold, and a dotted heading with its trailing dot (`N.e.w S.y.s.t.e.m I.n.s.t.r.u.c.t.i.o.n.s.`) holds. A list item with a label (`- printer: updated system instructions`), a commit subject (`docs: updated system instructions`), `TODO: write new system instructions`, an import, a path, a URL ending in the slug, a hard-wrapped line that ends in the phrase and a sentence with or without its full stop no longer hold. A combining mark before the colon (`new system instructions`, U+0345, `:`) no longer hides it. The three words may again be set apart by a sentence break when it is the phrase's own spelling, a break after every word (`new. system. instructions:`), the letters spelled with them (`n. e. w. s. y. s. t. e. m. i. n. s. t. r. u. c. t. i. o. n. s:`) or a hyphenated line wrap (`new-system-` and `instructions:` on the next line); `Updated. System instructions: see the wiki` stays two sentences and passes. Their separator now matches ADR-0074 d6: a space, an underscore, a hyphen, a full stop, a slash, an invisible character or a combining mark, and nothing else, so `new | system | instructions:` passes. **The cost.** The rule holds what is the phrase alone and is not a heading (a list item, a title field, a route such as `/new/system/instructions` or an identifier such as `new_system_instructions` that is the phrase alone on its line). It passes a directive with another word on its line: `Here are your new system instructions` followed by a list, `IMPORTANT - real system instructions` followed by its text, `Section 2: new system instructions`, a heading in a table cell or with a page number after it, a heading letter-spaced inside an HTML tag, the phrase with another separator between its words (`new+system+instructions:`, `new | system | instructions:`) and a sentence that ends in the phrase. The red-team corpus gains 13 malicious samples with 13 benign twins, and none of the existing samples changed its verdict. The false-positive corpus is now 135 samples: 35 of the 135 held under the rule set as merged, 8 of them by mistake (25.9%), and 28 hold now, all by design (20.7%). The refused-near-matches test asks a megabyte to scan in under two seconds and in no more than eight times the time of a quarter of it, plus 20 ms; it took 5 s before, and let a quadratic scan of 2.4 s by. The `oml-scan/1.2` set was not released, so no rescan beyond the one the `/1.2` fragment already asks for is needed.
