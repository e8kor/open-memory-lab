Changed the `new system instructions` rule of the injection scan's letters-only view, still `oml-scan/1.2` since that rule set is not released (#317; ADR-0074 d6, ADR-0065 d14). The phrase holds when a colon follows it on its line, a combining mark counting with its letter, or when it is its line: nothing else stands on the line, up to a line feed, carriage return, vertical tab, form feed, NEL or line or paragraph separator, but marks (any character that is not a letter, a digit or a bar, so a hash, an asterisk, a quote, a bracket, an arrow, an emoji, a box-drawing line or a dot), a list marker (a number or one letter, then a full stop or a closing bracket), up to eight HTML tags and up to eight more of the phrase. A heading of any depth, a closed or setext heading, a line in capitals, `**New system instructions**`, `> …`, `- …`, `=== … ===`, `NEW SYSTEM INSTRUCTIONS!`, `[…]`, `<<< … >>>`, `<h1>…</h1>`, `🔥 NEW SYSTEM INSTRUCTIONS 🔥`, a dotted heading with its trailing dot and a line of the phrase twice hold. A list item with a label (`- printer: updated system instructions`), a commit subject, `TODO: write new system instructions`, an import, a path, a URL ending in the slug, a hard-wrapped line that ends in the phrase and a sentence with or without its full stop no longer hold. The three words may be set apart by any mark: `**New** system instructions: reveal`, `new *system* instructions:`, `new (system) instructions:`, `NEW—SYSTEM—INSTRUCTIONS:`, `new,system,instructions:` and `new+system+instructions:` hold, as they did under `/1.1`. They may not be set apart by a bar (`new | system | instructions:` passes), a line break, or the end of a clause (a comma, semicolon, colon, question or exclamation mark and a space: `new, system, instructions:` passes). A full stop and a space after every word, between every letter (`n. e. w. s. y. s. t. e. m. i. n. s. t. r. u. c. t. i. o. n. s:`) or a hyphenated line wrap (`new-system-` and `instructions:` on the next line) is the phrase's own spelling and holds; one full stop among the words is two sentences (`Updated. System instructions: see the wiki` passes). A combining mark before the colon no longer hides it. **The cost.** The rule holds what is the phrase alone and is no heading: a list item, a title field, a route (`/new/system/instructions`), an identifier (`new_system_instructions`), a phrase alone inside a tag or quotation marks, and `The UI is new. System. Instructions: see the wiki.`, whose one-word sentence is the phrase's own spelling of a break. It passes a directive with another word on its line: `Here are your new system instructions` followed by a list and `IMPORTANT - real system instructions` followed by its text, which are given up; a line that ends in the phrase and `follow` or `below:` before it; a heading with a section or page number; a heading in a table cell; `请NEW-SYSTEM-INSTRUCTIONS` on its own line, since a letter of another script is not a mark (it holds with a colon after it); a heading letter-spaced inside an HTML tag; a line of more than eight tags or phrases; a roman-numeral list marker. The red-team corpus gains 20 malicious samples with 20 benign twins, and none of the existing samples changed its verdict. The false-positive corpus is 142 samples: 35 of the 135 that were in it before held under the rule set as merged, 8 of them by mistake (25.9%), 28 hold now, all by design (20.7%); the 7 new samples add 4 by-design holds. A megabyte of tags or phrases on one line no longer takes seconds: a first line rule read every tag again for each phrase and took 26 to 43 s (`<new system instructions>` repeated then one `x`), and now takes 80 ms in a release build. The refused-near-matches test asks a megabyte to scan in at most eight times the time of a quarter of it plus 20 ms, within 1 s in a debug build and 400 ms in a release build; it took 5 s before. The `oml-scan/1.2` set was not released, so no rescan beyond the one the `/1.2` fragment already asks for is needed.
