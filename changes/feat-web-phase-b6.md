- **One memory, Curate and the diagram frame move to `oml-web`** ([ADR-0070](adr/ADR-0070-the-web-console-is-a-client-of-the-public-http-api-in-a-crate-that-cannot-reach-the-application-core.md) d1, d5, d7 (b); ADR-0045 d10, d12, d21; ADR-0056 d9; issue #189). One memory opened by its id is at `/web/memory?id=<id>`, and on the admin page, with its attribution, its verdicts and the form that proposes its next version or its retirement, at `/web/admin/memory?id=<id>`; the admin's Curate view is at `/web/admin/curate`. The supersession and the curation run through the documented API operations as the person's curator or reviewer key, never the admin key, and a form sent twice runs once. A Mermaid diagram is drawn beside its source on every page that shows one, and now on the Review card too, so a reviewer who is not the author sees the drawing before the verdict; the frame and its pinned bundle are served by `oml-web` at `/web/render/` and `/web/assets/`. The admin read names each version's `kind`. The server answers the old `?view=memories&memory=<id>` and `?view=curate` addresses with a redirect carrying only the memory's id, and an old form's supersede or curate act with a redirect, running nothing. Caddy, both compose routers and the chart's `web.ingressPaths` route the three paths and the two prefixes; upgrading a deployment means adding them to the proxy ([Run the web console](operations/web-console.md)). A curator designation given during a session takes effect at the next sign-in, as every capacity of a session does.
