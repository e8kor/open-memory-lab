**Skills, documents and test cases are claim kinds, and a held version can be withdrawn.** `POST /v1/memories` and supersession take `kind: skill` with `skill {name, description}`, `kind: document` with an optional `document {title}`, and `kind: test` with `test {shape}`. Each object is signed with the version, stored beside it and served on every read of it. A skill is `text/markdown`, which a request that names no format gets by default, at most 32,768 bytes, with no front matter. Its claim key is derived as `skill/<name>`, and a successor keeps the name. A document's key is `doc/` and plain segments, sent or derived from its title by the heading slug rule (a long title keeps 47 characters and a digest), and the title defaults to the first level-1 heading, cut to 200 characters. A test case's key is `test/<name>`. Each field outside its grammar is refused `request.validation` naming it. The kinds follow their classes (a skill is an instruction, a document and a test case are knowledge). They are written over the HTTP API only: `oml_write` refuses them until their serving lands. Migrations 0048 and 0049 add the four columns, the `withdrawn` state and the `document_passages` table, adding their checks `NOT VALID` and validating them outside a transaction. A withdrawn version releases its claim key and has no live search postings.
