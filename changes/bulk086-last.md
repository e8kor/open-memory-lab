**A confirmation of an admin act requires a reason.** `POST /v2/admin/acts/{act}/confirm` takes a required `reason`, scanned and kept with the act as `confirm_reason`; one sent without is refused `request.validation` (`details.field: /reason`), and the console's confirm screen and `oml admin confirm --reason` send it ([ADR-0097](adr/ADR-0097-every-admin-act-is-one-journalled-record-narrowing-takes-effect-at-once-widening-waits-on-a-second-admin-person-and-every-admin-key-belongs-to-a-person.md) d26, the operator's answer 18 of 2026-11-27). Migration 0075 adds the column.

**A key records the person its issuer counted as.** At issuance a key keeps `issuer_person`, the person the issuing key counted as then, journalled on `key.issued`; a user key that counts as its issuer settles a fix as that person too, so relinking the issuer to another person afterwards no longer lets the keys it minted confirm or revert the first person's fix (#508 N1, the operator's answer 17 of 2026-11-27). Migration 0075 adds the column.
