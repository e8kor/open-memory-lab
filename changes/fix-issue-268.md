- **The widen hint's builds take turns by tenant, and the lead keeps its memory bound** ([ADR-0072](adr/ADR-0072-recall-searches-the-trusted-workareas-a-key-already-reads-chosen-per-query-by-reviewed-descriptors-and-never-widens-what-it-may-read.md) d8, d12; issue #268). The lead used to build signals strictly in the order recalls asked for them, so one tenant could queue many builds ahead of another tenant's first. Now each tenant with a signal waiting gets one build before any tenant gets a second. A signal over its tenant's share on its own now evicts the oldest signals, so the lead stays within its 8,000,000-pair bound. The stopped-build timeout now runs inside the refresh the transport spawns, and a test covers that path. The guard that keeps every commit going through the helper that drops signals now scans all of the app's sources and also catches a commit through a local binding or a fully qualified call. ADR-0072 now says that the 25 s database limit applies to each statement: when a build is stopped, its last statement can keep running for up to 25 s more.
