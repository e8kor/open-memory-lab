- **Reviewed descriptors do not earn their selection slots on the recall eval** ([ADR-0072](adr/ADR-0072-recall-searches-the-trusted-workareas-a-key-already-reads-chosen-per-query-by-reviewed-descriptors-and-never-widens-what-it-may-read.md), Phase 2 gate as measured; issue #206). `EVAL_PHASE2=1 eval/recall/federated.sh` measures phase 2's `described` selector against phase 1's `content` before any of phase 2 is built. It reads each query's union ranking from the served lead and replays selection from it, and the replay reproduced the served `wide` answer on all 385 queries. On the cross and skew splits together, success@5 rises 0.41 points on `bm25-postings` and on `bm25-sparse` (95% interval 0.00 to 1.24), where d11's floor is 3. An oracle filling every slot gains at most 0.83 points, and the adversarial descriptor lowers selection recall on 5 skew queries where d11 allows one, so `described` is not admitted. The record gains the readings the measurement took: the split, author-written descriptors, the descriptor score, the replay, α and the interval.
