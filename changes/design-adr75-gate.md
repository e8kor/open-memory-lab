- **ADR-0075's phase 0 gate now asks whether generated questions beat or add to `bm25-sparse`** ([ADR-0075](adr/ADR-0075-each-approved-memory-is-indexed-by-the-questions-it-answers-generated-off-the-request-path-scanned-stored-with-their-generator-and-matched-only-by-an-opt-in-pipeline.md) d12, the operator's decision of 2026-11-11, for [#235](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/235)). The BEIR, LoCoMo and displacement arms move to phase 2, the budgets stay, and the figures stay provisional until a person checks 10% of the grown set's labels.
