- **A distributed provider: one lead signs, nodes serve the workareas placed on them** ([ADR-0073](adr/ADR-0073-a-leader-signs-every-write-and-nodes-serve-the-workareas-placed-on-them-each-read-routed-to-the-closest-less-busy-replica-fresh-enough-for-it.md), proposed). The lead keeps every tenant's signing chain and every write; a node runs a PostgreSQL of its own holding the placement roots that weighted rendezvous hashing gives it, R per root spread across zones, plus the tenant's spine. Replication ships the signed journal and each node verifies every event's chain link, signature and body digest before it applies it. A read goes to a healthy, leased replica fresh enough for the client's position token, nearest tier first and the less busy of two random picks by peak EWMA, and to the lead when none qualifies. A read lease and a narrowing barrier make a revoked grant, a denial, a demotion or an erasure apply on every node before its caller hears it took effect. A federated recall (ADR-0072) gathers statistics, selects, then scatters to the nodes holding its sources and says which answered. Amends ADR-0023 d13 and d16, ADR-0024 d9, ADR-0002 d2 and ADR-0042 d1 and d4; three phases tracked as issues [#209](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/209), [#210](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/210) and [#211](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/211); a Cluster page mockup, not built.
