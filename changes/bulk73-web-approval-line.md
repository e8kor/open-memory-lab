**Web console.** A memory's card says when it was approved and whether a person or a reviewer agent approved it, in place of "approved by a key outside its lineage" (ADR-0077 d2, #175). A person approving their own agents' write is marked a self-review. A card the API serves no approval for, such as a shared `universe` row or the admin list, says "approved" and nothing more; an unreviewed card names no approval. The Rust SDK's `Memory` carries `approved_at` and `approver`.
