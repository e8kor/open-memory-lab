**Contributor and submitter replace writer.** [ADR-0093](adr/ADR-0093-contributor-and-submitter-replace-writer.md), accepted on 2026-11-19, renames the grant role `writer` to `submitter` and splits the key role into `contributor`, a key that recalls and writes with at least one reader and one submitter grant, and `submitter`, a key that writes and reads only its own rows. Existing writer keys become contributor keys, and `oml doctor` names any without a reader grant, to be reissued as a submitter key; no act changes a key's role. Imports, the user-root workspace create and the console's issue form take contributors only. Signed events keep `writer` and are read through the former-name map of [ADR-0091](adr/ADR-0091-workareas-become-workspaces-and-the-dreamer-role-becomes-reviewer-on-every-surface-in-one-cut-and-the-journals-former-names-are-read-through-one-map.md). `writer` becomes unknown input on every surface and `--write-only` is retired. The cut is planned for v0.8.4. Nothing here is built.
