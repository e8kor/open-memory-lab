- **The session gate resolves a capacity credential in one store call** ([ADR-0070](adr/ADR-0070-the-web-console-is-a-client-of-the-public-http-api-in-a-crate-that-cannot-reach-the-application-core.md) d2, d8, the table of 2026-11-12 for #297; [#297](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/297)). The session, the session key, its access and, for `<user>-web`, its root and the person's keys are read in one transaction under one snapshot where they were four to six transactions, and the gate makes every check it made on those rows. On a `GET` the request's first resolution of the caller decides on the access the gate read; a request with a body, and every later resolution, reads the access when it runs, as before. `whoami` without the person block reads 2 store calls for a session key where it read 9. No operation, field or page call changed, and who may read what is unchanged. Measured on a loaded host, the memory page, Keys, Start, People and the admin Atlas are within the 5 ms budget at 6 and at 48 workareas; Users is within it at 6 and 0.3 ms over at 48, and the admin Workareas view still misses it at 6.
