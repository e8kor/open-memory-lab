**Accepted: search by every claim kind, and a read without a query is the catalog.** On 2026-11-15 the operator accepted [ADR-0092](adr/ADR-0092-search-filters-by-every-claim-kind-and-ranks-skills-and-guardrails-as-data-and-a-read-without-a-query-is-the-catalog-of-what-a-key-reads.md), planned for v0.8.0 and not built. It records three decisions and twelve answers, each at its recommendation. Recall's `kinds` takes all ten kinds. A recall that names none returns hits of every kind the key reads, each marked with its kind; vocabulary appears only where a workspace opted in. `kinds` decides whether the steering list is served, and steering is never ranked. Offered skills and applicable guardrails rank as data hits: a skill hit carries its name, description and URI, a guardrail hit its text and rule, and neither runs or binds. One pipeline ranks every kind over BM25 statistics fixed before the filter, restating ADR-0049 d7, with one `k` and `matched_by_kind`. `oml_read`, `GET /v1/memories` and `oml read` without a query become the catalog: `kinds`, `prefix` and `state` filters; entries without bodies unless `include_text` is set; a 160-character preview; per-kind counts on the first page, capped by `catalog.count_cap`; held items marked unreviewed or needing a person; a release's members for a `latest` or pinned key. `own` now defaults to `false`, so an agent lists its own work with `own: true`. The console gains a Catalog page after Memories, with no admin twin. The record amends ADR-0025 d8, ADR-0037 d5 and d18 Q7, ADR-0039 d4, ADR-0049 d2 and d7, ADR-0052 d2 and d5, ADR-0080 d10 and d16, ADR-0081 d4 and ADR-0083 d12.
