**Web console and API.** A person whose `<user>-web` key carries a denial keeps their export and their deletion request (#335; ADR-0070 d2, a decision the operator took on 2026-11-12: "Keep both"). `GET /v2/web/export` and `POST /v2/web/deletion-requests` read nothing of the key's grants or denials, and now a test holds each: with a denial on `universe`, on the person's root and on a workarea outside it, the export answers `200` with what it served before the denial, and the deletion request journals `web.deletion_requested` under the denied key; the Start page draws the export link and the deletion form for that person and downloads and files through them. Both widen no access: the export serves what the person already wrote in their own root, and the deletion request asks for less. The alternatives the operator was offered, keeping the deletion request and refusing the export, and refusing both, are recorded in ADR-0070 with the decision. No behaviour changed. The sentence the Keys and Start pages show a denied person says that export, deletion and sign-out still work, and the administering guide and the roles-and-keys page say so too.
