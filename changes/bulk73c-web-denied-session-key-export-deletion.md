**Web console and API.** A person whose `<user>-web` key carries a denial keeps their export and their deletion request (#335; ADR-0070 d2, a decision the operator took on 2026-11-12, "Keep both", and confirmed the same day). `GET /v2/web/export` and `POST /v2/web/deletion-requests` read nothing of the key's grants or denials, before or after the decision, and now tests hold each: with a denial on `universe`, on the person's root and on a workarea outside it, the export answers `200` with what it served before the denial, and the deletion request journals `web.deletion_requested` under the denied key; the Start page draws the export link and the deletion form for that person and downloads and files through them. The export serves every memory in the person's root and below, every state, and the person's keys. After a denial on `universe`, on the root or on a workarea inside it, that includes text the same key is refused through `GET /v1/memories/{artifact_id}` (`access.not_found`): a test holds a memory in `alice.notes` in the export of a key denied there, text and all, beside the `404` for the read by its id. A denial on `<user>-web` denies none of the person's agent keys, and the deletion request ends nothing and changes no access. Without the two routes a person whose session key is denied has no way in the console to take their memories out or to ask for their account to end. The alternatives the operator was offered, keeping the deletion request and refusing the export, and refusing both, are recorded in ADR-0070 with the decision. No behaviour changed. The administering guide and the roles-and-keys page say the two still work.
