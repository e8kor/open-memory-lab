- **The recall eval runs in one command on your own machine** ([measure recall on your build](operations/recall-eval.md)). `eval/recall/selfhost.sh` builds the lead and the CLI, fetches the reranker's and the sparse encoder's pinned files with their digests checked, and runs the served eval over the eight pipelines CI measures against a throwaway PostgreSQL, naming any missing tool before it builds.
