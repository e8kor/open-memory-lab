- **The landing pages describe the scan as it ships in `oml-scan/1.1`** ([ADR-0065](adr/ADR-0065-the-scan-is-a-versioned-compiled-in-rule-set-that-vetoes-leaked-keys-and-holds-what-a-reviewer-should-judge.md)). The home, teams and privacy pages and the review-gate diagram said only "secrets". They now say that a private key, a known provider's API token, a `user:password` pair in a URL or after `curl -u`, a password set in config or a field over 1 MiB refuses the write, and that prompt-injection phrases, hidden Unicode and long base64 blobs are recorded as findings on the held memory. The diagram's scan label reads "secrets · injection".
