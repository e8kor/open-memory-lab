- **Recall may search the trusted workareas a key already reads** ([ADR-0072](adr/ADR-0072-recall-searches-the-trusted-workareas-a-key-already-reads-chosen-per-query-by-reviewed-descriptors-and-never-widens-what-it-may-read.md), proposed). A `committed` or `validated` workarea outside every in-progress subtree is a trusted source, with no new flag; a workarea advertises itself through a reviewed `descriptor` claim written and reviewed on the workarea it describes; a recall's `sources` argument, default `scope`, adds the readable trusted sources (`trusted`, ranked by CORI over the per-workarea counts search already keeps) or the ones whose descriptors match the query (`auto`), searched as one ranking with statistics summed over the nodes searched. Advertising grants nothing, and no key learns of a workarea it cannot read; discovery without read is a directory workarea. Three phases, each closed by leakage tests, are tracked as issues [#205](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/205), [#206](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/206) and [#207](https://github.com/e8kor/open-memory-lab/issues/207).
