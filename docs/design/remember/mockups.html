Open Memory Lab design mockups ADR-0050 · proposed 2026-11-04

Design artefact, not a built feature

Remember once, replace what changed, and know how it will be found

Today a write is checked for secrets and nothing else. A fact OML already holds becomes a second claim, a changed value sits beside the old one, and the writer cannot see which words will find it. ADR-0050 checks each write against what the writer may already read, lets a subject's next version replace the last, and answers with the terms it indexed.

Memory texts come from the remember eval (eval/remember/, version 1, synthetic). The chart's numbers are its offline run of 2026-11-04 and are upper bounds. Every response field, page layout and identifier on this page is illustrative.

ADR-0050 d16 · remember eval v1, offline, synthetic

The remember eval

40 seed memories, 24 writes (8 repeats, 8 changed values, 8 new), 24 paraphrased queries and 8 “latest” queries. One model wrote all of it, so these are upper bounds and an order; version 2, from real writes and independent queries, is the gate.

Writes that leave two live claims on one subject lower is better

A fresh write in the top 5 for a paraphrase higher is better

“Latest” queries that rank the replaced value first lower is better

ADR-0050 d14

The build, in increments

  1. W1
    The claim: upsert by claim_key, revise or withdraw one's own held version, history on request, reject as duplicate.

    no search dependency · gate: stale first 0 on a live lead · v0.5.3 proposed

  2. W2
    Findability: title, keywords, validity, sources, indexed in every answer.

    after ADR-0048 S1 · gate: +0.10 paraphrase success@5 · v0.5.3 proposed

  3. W3
    The similar check at write and at review; on_similar; the skill.

    after ADR-0048 S1 · gate: hint recall ≥ 0.90, precision ≥ 0.80 for refuse · v0.5.3 proposed

  4. W4
    Advisory warnings in the scan.

    gate: false warnings ≤ 0.10 · not assigned

  5. W5
    Freshness: review_by, stale marks, confirm.

    structural tests · not assigned

  6. W6
    Batch of 20 and capture by the agent at a task's end.

    gate: within-batch hints at d1's recall · not assigned

  7. W7
    Links proposed with the write, ratified with it.

    after ADR-0046's claim edges · not assigned

What holds at every increment

The check reads only what the writer may read. No write replaces a reviewed fact without a second identity. Nothing is ranked by time or by review, and stale memories are marked, never hidden or deleted. Three MCP tools stay three.
