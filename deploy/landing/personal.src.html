{{GLYPH:gate}}

Yours alone

Your memories live in your own workarea, which other people's keys cannot read. A pasted password is refused, not saved.

Connect your phone in three steps

  1. Get a code. On your page, under Connect Claude on the web or a phone, issue a connector key and copy its code. It works for ten minutes.
  2. Add the connector. On claude.ai, open Customize > Connectors and add a custom connector at https://memory.doublegate.io/mcp.
  3. Paste and confirm. Paste the code on the page that opens. Every Claude app on that account, your phone included, can then use it.

Claude's Free plan allows one custom connector. Your memory text stays on the server you choose; its request logs carry none of it and are kept at most 30 days.
