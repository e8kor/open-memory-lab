# Open Memory Lab brand kit

The Atlas: Open Memory Lab is drawn as one star system on an engraved atlas plate. Every asset is
generated by [`scripts/brand/gen.py`](scripts/brand/gen.py) into
[`docs/assets/brand/`](docs/assets/brand/). Edit the script, not the SVGs. Colour and type values
are in [`docs/assets/brand/tokens.md`](docs/assets/brand/tokens.md).

## The idea

- **The foundation is the star at the centre.** It is steady, and everything else depends on its
  gravity: a complete memory for agents and crews that is promised not to break.
- **The lab is the system forming around it.** Shared vocabularies and whole datasets sit on
  their orbits. Fenced experiments circle inside their own closed boundary and never cross into
  the main system. A forming arc beyond the outer orbit marks what grows next.
- **Nothing takes an orbit unseen.** A new body waits at the entry point of an orbit until a
  companion star, a second observer, has sighted it. Every sighting stays on record.

## What we say

Brand surfaces (README header, social card, GitHub About, launch posts) speak about the product
and its promise only. They never name languages, databases, protocols, deployment targets or
tool integrations, and they never show the repository address. Those belong in the docs.

- **Tagline:** Steady at the centre. Forming at the edges.
- **Proposal:** Shared memory for AI agents and crews: a steady foundation where nothing joins
  until someone other than its writer has looked, and a lab designed to keep forming around it.
- **The foundation:**
  - A complete memory your agents can depend on, with a promise not to break it.
  - Nothing becomes shared until someone other than its writer has looked.
  - Every decision stays on record, from the first write to the last removal.
- **The lab:**
  - Designed for shared vocabularies, so crews mean the same thing by the same word.
  - Grows to include whole datasets brought in as governed memory, and relationships between memories.
  - Designed for fenced experiments whose results never leak into real memory.
- **Stance:** AI-first, human in the loop. Open source.
- **Alternate lines:** "Nothing takes an orbit unseen." · "Shared memory with gravity."

### Rules for copy

- The lab is designed and on the roadmap. Say "designed for" or "grows to include", never that
  it ships today. State no numbers about OML.
- Keep clear of a sibling product's voice. Don't use "claim under test", "held", "signed",
  "admission control", "gate" or "agentic memory lab" as headline or brand words. Describe the
  review in plain words: "someone other than its writer has looked", "a second look".
- The docs keep their own fixed vocabulary ([ADR-0018](docs/adr/ADR-0018-the-documentation-set.md)).
  This file governs brand surfaces only.

## The mark

A full orbit ring in the lab's ice, which is also the O of Open. The warm star sits off-centre
inside it, and a companion body, the second observer, sits in a small gap in the ring.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `oml-icon.svg`, `oml-icon-512.png` | Avatar, app icon, organisation picture |
| `oml-mark.svg` / `oml-mark-light.svg` | Mark on dark / light backgrounds |
| `oml-mark-mono.svg` | One colour, inherits `currentColor` |
| `favicon.svg` | Browser tab and docs site |
| `oml-lockup-dark.svg` / `oml-lockup-light.svg` | Mark, name and tagline |
| `oml-wordmark-dark.svg` / `oml-wordmark-light.svg` | Mark and name only |
| `readme-hero-dark.svg` / `readme-hero-light.svg` | README header; `<picture>` switches with the GitHub theme |
| `social-preview.svg` / `social-preview.png` | GitHub social card, 1280×640 |

Keep clear space of half the ring's width around the mark. Don't recolour the star and ring
independently, don't rotate the mark, and don't put the ring on a busy background.

## Colour and type

- **Warm star colour is the foundation only.** Don't use it for decoration.
- **Ice (on dark) and steel (on light) are the lab.** Every orbit and body of the lab uses them.
- **Dashed means forming:** what the lab grows to next.
- **Night** is the primary ground, lit by a warm glow from the star. Chart paper is the light ground.
- **Type:** Newsreader Display SemiBold for the wordmark and headlines, Inter for body and orbit
  labels, JetBrains Mono for plate captions. All three are SIL OFL. The generated SVGs outline
  text to paths, so they render with no fonts installed.

Exact values and contrast ratios are in [`tokens.md`](docs/assets/brand/tokens.md).
